unstrap now talks to the strap the way the Zepp app does: the same messages, in the same order, at the same times. Sleep is the strap's own result, as in Zepp, and a few things you see change with it.

## Sleep

- **Sleep is the strap's own night, as it sent it.** unstrap no longer works out a night from the strap's minute by minute signal when the strap's own result is missing or looks wrong. A night the strap left out stays empty, as it does in Zepp. Nights unstrap made that way before are removed when you update. Nights the strap sent, and the changes you made to them, stay.

## Health alerts

- **Auto blood oxygen** is a new switch on Health alerts. The strap then measures blood oxygen on its own while you rest.
- **The low blood oxygen alert needs Auto blood oxygen on**, as in Zepp. While it's off, the alert is greyed out and says so.

## Syncing

- **A sync when you open the app runs only if the last one was more than 2 minutes ago**, as in Zepp. Opening it sooner sends nothing to the strap and shows no sync bar.
- **Fewer syncs in the background.** Like Zepp, unstrap syncs in the background only now and then, and otherwise when you open the app. So the night's sleep, the widgets and the morning alert may wait until you open unstrap.
- **No sync during a workout**, so the strap's link stays free for heart rate.

## Workouts

- **Live heart rate starts and stops the way the strap expects.** It worked before by luck: the old messages were one step off.
- **After the strap drops out mid workout**, unstrap tells it again where the workout stands: running, paused or resumed. Before, a paused workout was briefly started again on the strap.
- **"Looks like you've stopped"** now checks with the strap first when it says your heart rate is back to normal.

## Connecting

- Connecting follows Zepp's steps and order, and no longer asks Android for a faster link, which Zepp only does for a firmware update.

## Upgrading

Installs over 3.0.1 and keeps your data, except the nights worked out from the minute signal. Android 11 or newer.
