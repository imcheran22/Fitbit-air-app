A polish release for how the app looks and moves. Nothing changes in how unstrap talks to the strap or stores your data. Thanks to @Cryptobitsbee for all of it.

## Moving between days

- **Detail pages move with the day.** On Recovery, Energy, Load and Active minutes, the big figure now counts to the next day's number, and the arcs sweep across. The last day's page stays until the new day is worked out, so it no longer flashes "No reading" in between.
- **The week strip fades between days**, and each day keeps its dot whichever day is open. Heart points marks only the days the strap recorded points for.

## The strap pill

- **The strap's state is drawn in the pill at the top.** Connected shows the battery as a green fill with the percent, amber when low. Charging flows along the pill with a bolt. Not synced is orange. Not connected shows the strap drawing, dimmed.

## Goals and bests

- **Every badge and best is a medal**, each group with its own shape and each badge with its own picture. Locked medals are drawn quiet. Tap one to see how it's earned, when you earned it or how far along you are.
- **A new user's Goals page no longer stays on "Loading".**

## Smaller things

- A black window behind the app, so there's no grey flash when it opens.
- Bigger tap targets for the search clear button, the exercise name tick, the live workout page dots and the strap pill.

## Upgrading

Installs over 3.0.3 and keeps all your data. Android 11 or newer.
