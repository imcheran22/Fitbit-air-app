## Formula review

Science review of `:core:analytics` (formula set version 1 -> **2**, `ANALYTICS_ALGORITHM_VERSION = 2`).
Every constant and its source is in `docs/EVIDENCE.md`; the formulas as coded are in `docs/FORMULAS.md`.
**No source could be opened** (PubMed and Europe PMC are outside the session's network allow-list), so
all citations are recalled and marked unverified there. Evidence grades: *validated* (named study,
stated population, known error), *reasonable heuristic* (published idea, this exact use not
validated), *Apex-specific choice* (calibration, reason given). Every hunk of the analytics diff was
reviewed; all were kept. Tests that pin each change are in `core/analytics/src/test/...`.

| Engine | Finding | Severity | Fix / status | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training load (`Ewma.normalizedSeries`, `TrainingLoadEngine`) | ATL/CTL were seeded with the first recorded day. After 14 days of a 28-day average that day still carried 39 % of the weight, so a rest day (or a race) at the start of the history distorted CTL for weeks: 0 then 13 days of 50 TRIMP gave ATL 48.8 / CTL 30.3 = 1.61 ("high fatigue risk") for steady training | High | Fixed: normalised weights `y = sum d^i x / sum d^i`, no seed. Tests: `a doubled week ...` (explicit weighted mean, ATL 74.662, CTL 57.150), `a rest day at the start ...` (ratio 1.0387, hand-computed 49.698/47.846), `a constant load gives exactly that load`, `normalised ewma does not depend on a seed` (converges to the recursive EWMA on a long history) | reasonable heuristic (EWMA from Williams 2017; the initialisation is an Apex choice) |
| Training load (week-over-week change) | Compared weekly sums, so missing wear time looked like a training drop (a prior week with 3 recorded days gave +133 %; 6 of 7 days gave +17 % at an unchanged load) | Medium | Fixed: relative change of the mean recorded daily load, null unless both weeks have 5 recorded days. Test: `week over week change needs both weeks mostly recorded ...` | Apex-specific choice |
